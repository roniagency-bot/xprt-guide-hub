Common Questions

Answers Before You Call

What is Garage Liability Insurance?

It is business liability coverage written for automotive operations. It responds to bodily injury and property damage arising out of your garage business — your location, your operations, and the autos your business owns or uses. It generally does not cover damage to a customer's vehicle left in your care; that is what Garagekeepers is for.

What is Garagekeepers Coverage?

Garagekeepers covers physical damage to vehicles that belong to your customers while those vehicles are in your care, custody or control — sitting on your lot, in your service bay, or out on a test drive. Common causes of loss include fire, theft, vandalism and collision. It can be written on different bases, which change when and how it pays, so the form matters.

Can XPRT help with both insurance and a dealer bond?

Yes. We work on insurance and surety bonds, so a dealer, repair shop or body shop can handle both through one agency. Bond approval and pricing still go through underwriting and depend on the applicant, the bond type and the documentation provided.

What information is needed to receive a quote?

Usually the legal business name and address, what the business actually does day to day, years of experience, number of employees and payroll, vehicles the business owns, the value of inventory or customer vehicles on site, your loss history, and a copy of any current policy. We will tell you exactly what applies to you.
