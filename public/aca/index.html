What changed for 2027

This year, auto-renew isn't a plan.

Here's what we're seeing in Nevada and Colorado. We'll check how each one affects you.

Both states

The subsidy cliff is back

Extra federal help ended after 2025. Above 400% of the poverty level (about $63,840 for one person), there's no tax credit at all.

Colorado

Cigna is leaving Colorado

Cigna stops selling individual plans in 2027. Members can pick a new plan, with a special window until March 1, 2027.

Both states

Auto-renewal can cost you

You may be renewed automatically, but your price, network, and financial help can change. A quick review can catch it.

Both states

New rules for some immigrants

From 2027, tax credits are limited to citizens, green card holders, and a few other groups. We'll explain your options in plain language.

Sources: Connect for Health Colorado, Nevada Health Link, Healthinsurance.org. Rules can change; we confirm your details on the call.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is this the government Marketplace?

No. We are a private, licensed insurance agency. We are not affiliated with HealthCare.gov, Nevada Health Link, or Connect for Health Colorado. We help you shop and enroll through your state's official Marketplace.

Does it cost anything to talk with you?

No. The review is free. Agents are usually paid by the insurance company you choose, not by you.

Will you give me a price on this website?

No. This site sets up a conversation. Your agent shows you real plans and prices from the Marketplace once we know your details.

Do you sell my information?

No. Your request goes only to our agency. We don't sell or share it with other agents or lead buyers.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Puede hacer todo el proceso en español, desde el formulario hasta la llamada.

What if Open Enrollment is over?

You may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period after events like losing coverage, moving, getting married, or having a baby. Tell us what happened and we'll check.

Qué cambió para 2027

Este año, renovar en automático no es un plan.

Esto es lo que vemos en Nevada y Colorado. Revisamos cómo le afecta cada cambio.

Ambos estados

Regresó el límite de subsidios

La ayuda federal extra terminó después de 2025. Por encima del 400% del nivel de pobreza (unos $63,840 para una persona), no hay crédito fiscal.

Colorado

Cigna se va de Colorado

Cigna deja de vender planes individuales en 2027. Sus miembros pueden elegir otro plan, con un período especial hasta el 1 de marzo de 2027.

Ambos estados

La renovación automática puede costarle

Puede que lo renueven automáticamente, pero su precio, su red de médicos y su ayuda financiera pueden cambiar. Una revisión rápida lo detecta.

Ambos estados

Nuevas reglas para algunos inmigrantes

Desde 2027, los créditos fiscales se limitan a ciudadanos, residentes permanentes y algunos otros grupos. Le explicamos sus opciones en palabras sencillas.

Fuentes: Connect for Health Colorado, Nevada Health Link, Healthinsurance.org. Las reglas pueden cambiar; confirmamos sus datos en la llamada.
